Update Log
Latest update — October 22, 2026
  • Added the Global Company Market above the Bank in the Halloween version. The company starts with player investments only and has no artificial starting capital.
  • Added personal investment tracking: total clicks invested, current investment value, and overall profit/loss including earlier withdrawals.
  • Added compact square minus/plus minimize buttons to Global Company and Market activity, matching the My Titles panel controls.
  • Added a shared high-risk market with unpredictable booms, downturns, severe crashes, continuously changing investment values, inflation, an index history chart, recent market events, and anonymized public transaction summaries.
  • Investments now finish the named-save prompts before reserving clicks, reducing failed confirmations when a cloud save changes while a prompt is open.
  • Company investments now wait and retry when a cloud save/load is already running; genuine cloud conflicts get a clearer message before any clicks are reserved.
  • Investments reserve clicks securely and require creating or updating the player's named save to confirm. Canceling the named-save flow cancels the pending investment and returns the reserved clicks.
  • Added partial and full withdrawals at current market value, plus owner-only emergency controls to reset market conditions while preserving nominal investment values, add inflation, trigger a drop of up to 90%, and pause or resume market movement.
  • Added click and purchase sound effects.
  • Added a Settings toggle for Goofy Audio; turning it off stops current sounds and prevents new ones from playing.
  • Made sound effects avoid restarting while the same sound is still playing, and restored normal playback after removing a silence-detection change that muted the sounds.
  • Goofy Audio now defaults to Off for players without a saved On preference.
  • Added an optional fixed Mobile button in Settings. When enabled, a separate CLICK! button stays near the lower-right corner while scrolling and uses the normal click behavior.
  • Added a login screen before entering Ultimate Idle Clicker.
  • Added the Update Log, Suggestions, and player voting (Pulls) panels to the login screen.
  • Added a long-term Announcements board to the login menu, with larger pinned-notice cards, multiple saved announcements, and owner-only click-to-delete confirmation.
  • Added a Log Out button beneath Resume Game.
  • Kept the main login/menu card centered while placing the side panels to its right on desktop.
  • Added the Insomniac title.
  • Added the $$ title.
